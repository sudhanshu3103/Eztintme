Eligible Conditions for Medical Tint Exemptions

You may qualify for a medical window tint exemption if you have any of the following conditions:

  • Albinism
  • Dermatomyositis
  • Xeroderma pigmentosa
  • Porphyria
  • Severe drug-induced photosensitivity
  • Photophobia
  • Chronic actinic dermatitis/actinic reticuloid
